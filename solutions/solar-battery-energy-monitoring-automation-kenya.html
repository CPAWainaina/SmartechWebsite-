Solar Battery Monitoring & Energy Automation in Kenya

A solar system is easier to manage when the owner can see what the battery, inverter, solar array, grid and loads are doing—and receive an alert before a power problem becomes a business interruption.

What customers actually need to know

Searches such as “solar battery drains quickly,” “battery does not last overnight,” “solar system keeps switching to grid,” “how do I monitor my solar battery,” and “why is my inverter showing a fault?” are usually symptoms of a visibility problem as much as an equipment problem.

Monitoring turns an intermittent complaint into evidence: energy flow, battery behaviour, load demand, alarms and operating history.

Monitoring points

Battery

SOC, voltage, current, temperature, charge/discharge power and available alarms where exposed by the BMS.

Solar

PV power, daily energy, inverter status and fault information where available.

Grid

Grid presence, voltage, current, demand and energy imported where meters support the measurements.

Loads

Critical and non-critical load consumption, demand peaks and unusual changes.

Generator

Run status, load, alarms, runtime and fuel information where the controller or sensors provide it.

Alerts

Notifications for low SOC, inverter faults, prolonged outages, generator events and abnormal power conditions.

Automation around the battery

Monitoring becomes more useful when the system can respond. Depending on the installed inverter, BMS, generator controller and control hardware, automation can manage source priority, battery charging windows, generator start/stop signals, essential-load transfer and alarm escalation.

Important: automation should follow the manufacturer's supported control interfaces and the site's electrical protection design. It should not bypass BMS protections or create unsafe parallel-source conditions.

Remote monitoring for multi-site operations

Businesses with several branches, remote pumps, farms, security compounds or telecom sites can benefit from a common dashboard. Instead of waiting for a site to report “the power is off,” the operations team can receive an event and inspect the available telemetry before dispatching a technician.

Questions customers ask before calling

Why is my solar battery draining fast?

High loads, insufficient solar generation, battery condition, inverter settings, temperature, standby consumption and unexpected loads can all contribute. Monitoring helps separate these causes.

Can monitoring show battery health?

It can show the health indicators exposed by the battery BMS and inverter, but battery state-of-health estimates vary by battery technology and manufacturer.

Can battery monitoring trigger a generator?

Where the inverter/controller and generator support the required interface, battery SOC and grid status can be used as inputs to a controlled generator start/stop sequence.
