The problem behind the search

Customers often search “CCTV goes off when power goes out,” “CCTV for a farm without electricity,” “solar CCTV Kenya,” “remote CCTV without grid power,” or “how long will CCTV work on battery?” The answer is not simply a camera with a solar panel. The whole power and communications chain needs to be designed.

How the integrated system works

Solar PV

Generates energy for the surveillance system and other approved site loads.

Battery storage

Maintains camera and network operation outside solar-production hours and during outages.

Power protection

Appropriate protection, distribution and isolation help protect cameras, switches, recorders and communications equipment.

Network

PoE switching, fibre, wireless links or cellular connectivity can be selected around distance and site conditions.

CCTV

Camera coverage is designed around perimeter, entrances, assets, lighting, storage and operational risk.

Monitoring

Power and security events can be brought together so operators know whether a camera is offline because of power, network or equipment failure.

Power monitoring that helps security teams

A remote site should not silently lose surveillance. Useful alarms can include low battery SOC, inverter fault, mains failure, solar under-performance, camera offline, recorder fault, storage issue and communications loss.

This creates a practical chain: detect → alert → diagnose → respond. It is particularly valuable for farms, construction sites, warehouses, gated compounds, telecom sites and other locations where sending a technician is expensive.

CCTV + solar + generator where uptime matters

For larger sites, solar and batteries can be combined with grid and a generator so security equipment remains operational through longer outages. The power architecture can prioritise critical CCTV and network loads before non-essential loads, subject to the site's electrical design.

Questions customers ask before calling

Will CCTV work during a power outage?

Yes, if the cameras, recorder/network equipment and communications path have an appropriately designed backup supply. The runtime depends on load, battery capacity, system efficiency and operating conditions.

Can solar power CCTV at night?

Yes. Solar charges the battery during available sunlight, and the battery supplies the CCTV load when solar generation is insufficient.

Can I monitor both CCTV and power?

Yes, where the CCTV platform and power/energy monitoring system provide the required interfaces. The aim is to identify whether an outage is caused by power, networking or camera equipment.
