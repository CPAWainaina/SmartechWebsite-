Our technology and supply approach

Equipment, documentation and installation

Smartech works with established solar equipment manufacturers, distributors and suppliers to source equipment for project requirements. We publish supporting authorization and recommendation documents where their scope can be stated accurately. The Yangzhou Bright Solar Solutions authorization on this page, for example, relates to a January 2026 KCB clean-energy tender/EOI. The exact brand, model and quantity are confirmed during design and procurement. Product documents on this page are provided so customers and procurement teams can review the technical information available for selected equipment.

01

Assess the site

We look at the load, operating hours, roof or ground space, existing power sources and the required backup period.

02

Select the equipment

We match the inverter, battery, PV modules and protection equipment to the proposed system rather than choosing by product name alone.

03

Install and document

The installation includes wiring, protection, testing and handover information appropriate to the project scope.

Documented solar supply authorization

The same supplied document includes a manufacturer warranty statement for the goods offered.

MANUFACTURER

Yangzhou Bright Solar Solutions Co., Ltd.
