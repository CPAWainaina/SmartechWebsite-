Nationwide Coverage

Solar, CCTV, Electrical & Fibre Services Across All 47 Counties of Kenya

Smartech Technologies accepts project enquiries across Kenya, from Nairobi and major towns to rural, off-grid and remote locations. We design and deliver solar, hybrid power, CCTV, electrical, fibre, structured cabling and security infrastructure subject to site assessment, access, logistics and scope.

Request a Nationwide Site AssessmentSouth Sudan / Juba Projects
Kenya-wide service model: Our coverage is not limited to the county headquarters. For remote deployments, we plan mobilisation, transport, mounting, power autonomy, communications, security and commissioning around the actual site. This allows us to support projects in rural and hard-to-reach areas as well as urban centres.
