Lift Inspection in Kenya: Requirements, Preparation and What Facility Owners Should Know

A lift should not be treated as simply another building asset. It is a safety-critical machine with mechanical, electrical and control components that need maintenance, records and examination. Kenya's Occupational Safety and Health framework places requirements on hoists and lifts, while DOSHS provides plant registration and examination services.

What is lift inspection in Kenya?

There are two different activities that owners should distinguish: routine maintenance/condition checks and a statutory examination where the applicable law requires one. A competent maintenance team can identify defects, but a statutory examination must be completed by the appropriate approved person or institution.

What should be checked before an examination?

  • Lift identification, manufacturer and rated load
  • Previous examination reports and defect close-out records
  • Machine room and accessible equipment condition
  • Lift car, doors, landing doors and safety devices
  • Emergency alarm and communication arrangements
  • Electrical isolation, control panel and visible wiring condition
  • Safety circuits, interlocks and emergency stops where applicable
  • Safe access to the lift well, pit and other examination areas

Why pre-inspection readiness matters

A readiness assessment helps facility managers discover problems before an examination visit. It also makes it easier to give the examiner accurate plant records and safe access. Smartech can support electrical/control checks and corrective works within its competence and coordinate with the appropriate approved examiner.

How often should a lift be examined?

The Occupational Safety and Health Act provides for thorough examination of a hoist or lift at least once every six months, after modifications or extensive repairs, or within a shorter period where applicable. Owners should confirm the current statutory requirement for their specific plant and retain the prescribed reports.

What should a facility manager keep?

Maintain a plant file containing identification details, manufacturer information, maintenance records, examination reports, defects and corrective action evidence. Good records make compliance easier and help future technicians understand the equipment history.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

How often should a lift be examined in Kenya?

The OSHA framework provides for thorough examination at least every six months and after modifications or extensive repairs, subject to the applicable requirements.

Who can issue a statutory lift examination report?

The statutory examination should be performed by the appropriately approved person or institution under the applicable DOSHS requirements.
