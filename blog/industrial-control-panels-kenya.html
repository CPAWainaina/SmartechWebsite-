Industrial Control Panels in Kenya: Design, Components and Testing

A control panel is the physical point where power, protection, control logic and field signals meet. Poor panel design can create maintenance problems even when the PLC programme itself is correct.

What is inside an industrial control panel?

Depending on the application, a panel can contain isolators, breakers, contactors, overload relays, motor protection, VFDs, PLCs, power supplies, relays, terminals, network devices and HMI equipment.

Good panel design should consider

  • Load and short-circuit requirements
  • Heat dissipation
  • Segregation of power and control wiring
  • Labelling and terminal identification
  • Future expansion
  • Ingress protection and environment
  • Safe isolation and maintenance access

Testing before commissioning

Depending on the project, testing can include visual inspection, wiring verification, continuity checks, I/O simulation, protection checks, control logic tests and functional testing before site commissioning.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Can you retrofit an existing control panel?

Yes, where the enclosure, space, power and equipment condition allow a safe retrofit. A site survey is normally required.

Should PLC panels be documented?

Yes. Drawings, I/O schedules, labels and backups make future troubleshooting much easier.

What is an MCC?

A motor control centre is a panel assembly used to distribute and control power to multiple motors and associated equipment.

Can VFDs be installed in existing panels?

Potentially, but thermal, electrical, protection, EMC and space requirements must be checked first.
