Machinery Safety Checklist Kenya: 20 Checks Before You Start Work

A machinery checklist should be simple enough for operators and detailed enough for maintenance and safety teams. It is not a substitute for a statutory examination, but it can identify problems early.

20 machinery safety checks

  1. Machine identification is visible.
  2. Rated capacity is known.
  3. Guards are fitted and secure.
  4. Emergency stops are accessible.
  5. Safety interlocks operate as intended.
  6. Isolation points are labelled.
  7. Electrical panels are secure.
  8. No exposed live parts are accessible.
  9. Cables are protected.
  10. Earthing/bonding arrangements are appropriate.
  11. Hydraulic or pneumatic leaks are absent.
  12. Moving parts show no abnormal damage.
  13. Bearings and mechanical parts are maintained.
  14. Controls are clearly labelled.
  15. Warning signs are visible.
  16. Operators are trained.
  17. Maintenance records are current.
  18. Previous defects are closed.
  19. Modifications are documented.
  20. Required statutory examinations are current.

Turn the checklist into a management system

Use equipment IDs, inspection dates, defect severity, responsible persons and due dates. For multi-site businesses, central reporting makes overdue actions easier to control.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is this checklist a statutory certificate?

No. It is a practical safety checklist and does not replace any legally required examination or certificate.

Who should carry out statutory examinations?

The appropriate DOSHS-approved person or institution should carry out prescribed statutory examinations.

Can the checklist be used for vehicle lifts?

Yes as a pre-inspection aid, but the lift manufacturer and applicable statutory requirements should also be followed.

Should defects be recorded?

Yes. Record the defect, risk, action, owner and close-out evidence.
