What to check before replacing equipment

When a customer says “the battery is not lasting,” the first question should be what the battery is actually doing. Monitoring can show the relationship between solar generation, load demand and battery charge/discharge.

Useful monitoring points

  • Battery state-of-charge
  • Battery voltage and current
  • Charge/discharge power
  • Temperature and available BMS alarms
  • Solar generation
  • Grid availability and import
  • Load demand
  • Inverter status and faults
  • Generator status where integrated

From monitoring to automation

Where the equipment supports it, battery SOC and grid status can become control inputs for generator start requests, load prioritisation and alarm escalation. The BMS protections must remain authoritative.
