How Often Must a Hoist or Lift Be Thoroughly Examined in Kenya?

Section 63 of Kenya’s Occupational Safety and Health Act requires a hoist or lift to be thoroughly examined at least once every six months, or after modifications or extensive repairs, by a person approved for the purpose. The applicable legal exception for a non-mechanically powered hoist or lift changes the interval to twelve months.
