Electric Motor Vibration and Noise: Causes and Checks

A practical guide to investigating vibration, bearing noise, alignment, mounting and driven-load problems. Smartech Technologies approaches motor projects as complete systems: motor, driven load, starter or drive, protection, cabling, controls and operating conditions all matter.

What should be assessed?

Start with the motor nameplate, supply voltage, rated current, speed, duty, driven equipment and operating environment. Then review the starting method, protection, cable route, control requirements and maintenance history. For pumps, fans and similar variable-torque loads, the operating profile is especially important when evaluating a VFD.
