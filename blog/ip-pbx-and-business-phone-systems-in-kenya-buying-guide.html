IP PBX and Business Phone Systems in Kenya: Buying Guide

A practical, buyer-focused guide for Kenyan homes, businesses, factories, facilities and project teams.

What customers should consider

Before buying or commissioning equipment, confirm the application, load or duty, site conditions, safety requirements, documentation, maintenance plan and total cost of ownership. Avoid choosing equipment only from a headline price.

For IP PBX Kenya, a site survey is often the fastest way to turn a general question into an accurate scope. Smartech Technologies supports engineering, installation, integration, testing, commissioning and maintenance within the agreed technical scope.

Questions to ask your contractor

  • What exactly is included in the quotation?
  • Which equipment and specifications are being proposed?
  • What tests and commissioning will be completed?
  • What documentation will be handed over?
  • What preventive maintenance is recommended?
  • Which statutory examination or approval, if any, must be performed by an appropriately approved person?
