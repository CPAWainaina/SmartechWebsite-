What to check before replacing equipment

If a standby generator does not start when the power goes off, the problem may be the generator itself, the starting battery, the controller, fuel system, protection circuit or the ATS signal. Start with diagnosis rather than guessing.

Common symptoms and what they can point to

  • No crank: battery, charger, starter, control fuse, emergency stop or start circuit.
  • Cranks but does not fire: fuel supply, air, fuel solenoid, injection or engine-related issue.
  • Starts then stops: low-oil/coolant protection, overspeed/underspeed, sensor or controller alarm.
  • Starts manually but not automatically: ATS, mains-sensing, start signal or control wiring.

What should be tested?

A proper diagnostic visit should establish battery condition and charging, controller alarms, mains-failure signal, start command, fuel delivery, engine protection, generator output and ATS transfer sequence.
