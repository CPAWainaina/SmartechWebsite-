What to check before replacing equipment

Remote CCTV fails for more reasons than a bad camera. Power, battery capacity, PoE equipment, network connectivity, storage and communications all affect whether surveillance remains available.

Design around the complete load

Count cameras, IR/night power, PoE switches, recorder/NVR, router, radio or 4G equipment and any other connected load. Then size solar and battery capacity around the operating profile and required autonomy.

Add useful alarms

  • Low battery SOC
  • Solar/inverter fault
  • Camera offline
  • Recorder or storage fault
  • Network/communications loss
  • Mains failure where grid is present
