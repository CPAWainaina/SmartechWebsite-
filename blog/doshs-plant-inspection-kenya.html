DOSHS Plant Inspection in Kenya: Registration, Examination and Records

Kenya's Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health Services has responsibilities covering workplace and plant registration, inspection, examination and testing. Understanding the process helps owners avoid missing records or approaching the wrong type of inspection.

What does DOSHS do?

DOSHS provides occupational safety and health services including registration of plants, workplace inspection and examination/testing of plants. Its published information states that plant records include boilers, hoists and lifts, cranes and other lifting equipment, air receivers, steam receivers, refrigeration plants and compressed/liquefied gas cylinders.

Plant registration

DOSHS explains that plant registration uses the prescribed process and forms, with technical review and certification. Owners should keep plant information accurate and current.

Examination and testing

DOSHS states that approved persons conduct prescribed plant examinations and submit the relevant statutory forms for technical review. This is why businesses should distinguish a general engineering inspection from a statutory examination.

What should businesses maintain?

  • Plant register
  • Equipment identification
  • Manufacturer documentation
  • Maintenance records
  • Previous examination reports
  • Defect and corrective-action records
  • Modification history

Frequently asked questions

What is plant registration?

It is the process through which DOSHS records applicable plant and issues the relevant registration documentation.

Who examines plant?

DOSHS uses approved persons for prescribed examination and testing functions.
