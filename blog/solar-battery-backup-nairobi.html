Solar Battery Backup in Nairobi: When Is It Worth It?

Battery storage can improve continuity and reduce reliance on grid power, but the economics depend on load profile and required autonomy.

Start with the site, not the product

Before choosing equipment, document the property, loads, security risks, operating hours, connectivity and environmental conditions. A professional site survey turns a generic shopping list into a system specification.

What should the quotation contain?

Ask for equipment specifications, quantities, installation scope, protection and accessories, testing and commissioning, warranty terms, exclusions, maintenance options and the assumptions behind the design.

Questions to ask your contractor

  • What exactly is included in the installation?
  • How will the system be tested and handed over?
  • Which components have manufacturer warranty?
  • Who will support the system after installation?
  • What site conditions could change the price?

Kenya and South Sudan considerations

Projects across East Africa can face different logistics, connectivity, security and operating conditions. For South Sudan assignments, confirm site access, transport, local implementation requirements and support arrangements before procurement.
