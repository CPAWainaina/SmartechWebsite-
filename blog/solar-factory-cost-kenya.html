How Much Does Industrial Solar Cost for a Factory in Kenya?

Industrial solar pricing depends on system capacity, roof or ground installation, electrical integration, monitoring, batteries and project complexity. Here is how buyers should compare quotations.

  • Compare price per installed kWp only after confirming that the quotes include comparable equipment and scope.
  • Check whether structural works, electrical protection, cabling, monitoring and commissioning are included.
  • Battery storage should be priced and evaluated separately from PV generation.
  • Ask for expected annual energy generation and the assumptions used to calculate savings.
  • For large factories, consider phased installation if capital budget or production schedules make a single-stage project difficult.
