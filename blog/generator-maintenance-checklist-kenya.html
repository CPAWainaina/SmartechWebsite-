What to check before replacing equipment

A generator that starts once during a test is not automatically a reliable standby system. Maintenance should cover the engine, electrical output, controls, starting system and transfer equipment.

Maintenance areas

  • Engine oil, filters and lubrication
  • Coolant, radiator and cooling airflow
  • Fuel quality, filters, tank and leaks
  • Starting battery, terminals and charger
  • Alternator, AVR and output measurements
  • Controller alarms and protection
  • ATS/changeover operation
  • Load testing where appropriate
  • Service records and corrective recommendations

Why ATS testing matters

A generator may be healthy but still fail to supply the building if the mains-sensing or transfer circuit is faulty. Testing the complete sequence is therefore more useful than servicing the engine alone.
