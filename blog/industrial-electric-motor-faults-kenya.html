What to check before replacing equipment

For factories and commercial facilities, “the motor is faulty” is often only the visible symptom. The root cause may be the electrical supply, motor protection, starting method, driven equipment, mechanical condition or control system.

Common problem-led searches

  • Motor keeps tripping on overload
  • Three-phase motor is overheating
  • Motor draws high current
  • VFD keeps faulting
  • Pump pressure is unstable
  • Motor vibrates or makes unusual noise
  • Motor has high starting current

Why the application matters

A pump, fan, conveyor and compressor can have very different starting torque, duty cycles and control requirements. The correct solution may be a protection adjustment, mechanical correction, soft starter, VFD, sensor feedback, MCC change or a combination.
