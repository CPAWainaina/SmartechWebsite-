Lift & machinery inspection

Do you issue statutory lift certificates?

Statutory examinations must be performed by the appropriate approved person or institution where required. Smartech provides technical preparation and corrective support within its competence.

Yes. We can assess visible electrical/control and safety-readiness issues and help create a defect close-out plan.

Do you support automotive workshops?

Yes. Our automotive offering focuses on vehicle-lift inspection readiness, workshop electrical systems, machinery and control infrastructure.

Projects

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Yes. We undertake projects across Kenya and selected regional assignments depending on logistics and scope.

Can you handle multi-site projects?

Yes. We can standardise site surveys, reporting, installation and maintenance workflows across multiple locations.

Compliance

Where can I verify DOSHS information?

Use the official State Department for Labour and Skills Development resources for current forms, approved persons and regulatory information. Smartech can help interpret the technical preparation requirements.

No. A maintenance or readiness check should not be presented as a statutory examination where legislation requires an approved examiner.
