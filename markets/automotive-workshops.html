FAQ

Common automotive workshop questions

Do vehicle lifts need inspection?

Where a lift falls within plant subject to statutory examination, the owner should arrange the required examination by the appropriate approved person and keep the prescribed records.

Can you inspect workshop machinery?

We can perform technical condition and inspection-readiness assessments within our competence and coordinate statutory examination requirements where applicable.

Can you repair defects found during inspection?

Yes, where the work falls within our electrical, control, security or infrastructure scope. Mechanical repairs should be assigned to the appropriate competent specialist.

Can you support multiple branches?

Yes. We can plan site surveys, standardised checklists, corrective works and documentation across multi-site operations in Kenya.
