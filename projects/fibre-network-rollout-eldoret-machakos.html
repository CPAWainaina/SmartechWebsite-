Fibre & Structured Cabling

Multi-County Fibre Optic Network Rollout: Eldoret & Machakos

Geotagged trenching, duct-laying and splicing works from a multi-county fibre optic rollout across Uasin Gishu and Machakos Counties.

Project record: This case study is based on project information supplied by Smartech Technologies, including on-site geotagged photographs. Where a client outcome, date, location or equipment model has not been provided, the website does not invent it.

Project scope

  • Fibre optic trenching and open-cut duct installation across multiple counties
  • HDPE duct laying, cable coiling and staging for a multi-site rollout
  • Fibre splice joint closure and termination works
  • Roadside excavation with traffic management and safety controls
  • CCTV camera pole installation on the same civil works corridor

Why this project matters

Fibre network rollouts require consistent execution across multiple sites and counties — correct trench depth, duct protection, proper backfilling, and clean splice terminations. Smartech's project record includes geotagged photos from works along Kitale Road in Eldoret (Uasin Gishu County) and Makutano–Kitui Road in Machakos County, documenting the trenching, duct and splicing standard applied across the rollout.

This project demonstrates Smartech’s capacity to deliver consistent, standards-compliant fibre trenching and splicing across multiple counties and sites — a core requirement for ISPs, telecom operators and enterprise network rollouts.
