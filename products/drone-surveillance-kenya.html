Drone surveillance & intrusion detection in Kenya

Drone-based surveillance adds an aerial layer to fixed CCTV and perimeter alarms for large sites — ranches, farms, industrial yards and estates — where ground-based cameras alone leave gaps. Deployment can be scheduled patrols or automatic dispatch triggered by a perimeter or intrusion alarm.

How does a drone respond to an intrusion alarm?

A drone-in-a-box or scheduled-patrol setup can be linked to your perimeter alarm, electric fence or CCTV analytics so that a triggered alarm zone is automatically flagged for aerial verification, reducing false-alarm callouts and giving faster visual confirmation over a large site than a ground patrol alone.
