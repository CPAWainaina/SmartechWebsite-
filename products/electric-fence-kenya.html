How much does electric fencing cost in Kenya?

There is no single useful price because capacity, equipment specification, installation conditions and site complexity change the total. Request an itemized quotation after a site assessment.

Do you install electric fencing for farms and ranches, not just homes?

Yes. Long-perimeter installations for farms and ranches use higher-output energizers, additional earthing and zone splitting so a single fault does not take down the whole perimeter. Send the approximate perimeter length and terrain and we can scope the right configuration.
