# Smartech Technologies — Citations & Off-Page SEO Action Plan

This is the half of "rank #1 in Kenya and East Africa" that cannot be done by editing website files. Google ranks local-service businesses on a mix of on-site content (which is now in good shape — see SEO-V9 and V10 changelogs) and **off-site trust signals**: citations, backlinks, reviews and a verified Google Business Profile. I cannot submit forms on third-party directories on your behalf — this is the exact list and copy to do it yourself, in priority order.

## 1. Lock your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) — do this first
Every citation below must use **byte-for-byte identical** business info, or it weakens rather than helps you. Use exactly:

```

Phone         : +254 701 427 045
Email         : info@smartechtech.co.ke
Website       : https://www.smartechtech.co.ke
Address       : [FILL IN — your real registered/office street address, Nairobi]
```

The website's schema markup currently uses "Nairobi, KE" only — send me your real street address and I'll update `index.html`'s `LocalBusiness` schema to match exactly what you submit to Google.

## 2. Google Business Profile (highest priority — do this before anything else)
This is the single biggest lever for "appearing as the solution when people search" in Kenya. Free, takes 20 minutes, verification takes longer (postcard or phone).
- Create/claim at business.google.com using the exact NAP above
- Category: primary = "Solar energy contractor", secondary = "Electrical installation service", "Security system installer", "Telecommunications contractor"
- Upload 15–20 of your real project photos (the ones now on the website are a ready-made set)
- List all service areas: Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Nakuru, Mombasa
- Post updates monthly (a finished project, a new service) — Google rewards active profiles
- Ask every satisfied client for a Google review with their company name included — review count and recency directly affect local ranking

## 3. Kenya & East Africa business directories (citations)
Submit the exact same NAP to each. Free tier is fine to start:
- Yellow Pages Kenya (yellowpageskenya.com)
- Tuugo Kenya (tuugo.co.ke)
- Business List Kenya (businesslistkenya.com)
- Kenya Yellow Pages (kenyayp.com)
- Brabys East Africa
- Yalwa Kenya
- Hotfrog Kenya
- Kenya Trade Directory (if KNCCI-affiliated)
- EABC / East African Business Council member directory (if eligible) — directly supports "East Africa" positioning
- Construction Kenya / BuildKenya (industry-specific — strong relevance signal for a contractor)

## 4. Industry/association credibility
- EPRA licensed contractor listing (if EPRA publishes a public register — verify and request inclusion)
- NCA (National Construction Authority) registration is already claimed on-site — confirm it appears on NCA's own public contractor register and link to it
- Kenya Renewable Energy Association (KEREA) membership, if applicable
- Consider a LinkedIn Company Page — not a citation in the classic sense, but ranks in Google for the business name and is where B2B/NGO procurement teams check credibility

## 5. Backlinks (harder, higher value)
- Ask the institutions/programmes named in your project pages (universities, NGOs, telecom partners) if they'll link to the case study from their own site or a press mention — even one link from a .ac.ke or .org domain outweighs dozens of directory listings
- Local news/business press: a short press release about a completed project (e.g. the Turkana solar electrification work) pitched to a Kenyan business or trade publication
- Supplier/manufacturer "certified installer" pages, if any of your suppliers (inverter/battery/CCTV brands) maintain a partner or installer directory
