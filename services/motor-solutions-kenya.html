Start, protect and control motors correctly

We help businesses assess motor-driven equipment and develop practical electrical and control solutions. Depending on the application, this can include direct-on-line starters, star-delta starting, soft starters, VFDs, motor protection, control panels, MCC integration and PLC/SCADA interfaces.

Industrial motor problems customers search for

Most customers do not search for “motor engineering” first. They search the symptom: motor keeps tripping, motor overheating, motor drawing high current, pump pressure is unstable, VFD keeps faulting, motor vibrates, motor will not start, or electricity consumption is too high.

Motor trips repeatedly

Check overload settings, phase imbalance, supply quality, mechanical load, bearings, starting method and motor condition.

Motor runs hot

Investigate loading, cooling, voltage imbalance, harmonics, bearing condition, ventilation and duty cycle.

VFD keeps faulting

Review fault history, motor data, acceleration/deceleration, cable installation, load behaviour and protection settings.

High starting current

Assess DOL, star-delta, soft-start or VFD options against the motor and driven equipment.

Pump pressure changes

Consider variable-speed control, sensor feedback, pump selection, pipe conditions and control logic.

High motor energy use

Measure operating demand and assess control, loading, throttling, power quality and efficiency opportunities.

Industrial motor integration with power and monitoring

Motor systems can be connected to MCCs, PLCs, SCADA, energy meters and remote monitoring. This allows operators to see motor status, run hours, faults, current, power and process conditions where the installed controls expose the data.

For sites using solar, generators or batteries, motor loads should be separated from critical loads and assessed for starting current and operating demand before being included in a backup-power strategy.
