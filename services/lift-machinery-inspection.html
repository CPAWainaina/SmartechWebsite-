Why this matters

Inspection is more than a certificate

Plant owners need equipment that is safe, maintained, documented and ready for examination. Kenya's DOSHS services cover registration of plant and examination/testing of plants including boilers, hoists and lifts, cranes and other lifting equipment, air receivers, steam receivers, refrigeration plants and gas cylinders.

Smartech can help identify visible electrical, control, access, documentation and maintenance issues before the formal examination.

Important compliance distinction

Where Kenyan law requires a statutory examination or certificate, the examination must be carried out by the appropriate DOSHS-approved person or institution. Smartech does not represent itself as a statutory plant examiner unless separately approved for that specific function.

We can provide technical inspection support, corrective works, documentation preparation and coordination with the approved examiner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Kenyan plant owners ask

How often should a lift be examined in Kenya?

The Occupational Safety and Health Act provides for thorough examination of hoists and lifts at least once in every six months, after modifications or extensive repairs, or within a shorter period where applicable. Always confirm the current statutory requirements and the examiner's instructions for the specific plant.

Does every machinery inspection require DOSHS?

Not every routine maintenance or condition assessment is a statutory DOSHS examination. Certain plant categories are subject to registration, examination and testing requirements. The correct requirement depends on the plant and applicable regulations.

Can Smartech issue a statutory lift certificate?

Only an appropriately approved person or institution can conduct the statutory examination and issue the prescribed report/certificate where required. We can support the technical preparation and corrective works.

What should I prepare before an inspection?

Prepare plant identification, previous examination reports, maintenance records, manufacturer information, drawings where available, certificates, defect close-out evidence and safe access to all relevant equipment areas.

Need a lift or machinery inspection assessment?

Send the equipment type, location, manufacturer/model and last inspection date. We can advise on the appropriate technical scope and whether a statutory examiner should be involved.
