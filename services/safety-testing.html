Frequently Asked

Common questions

What does an EPRA safety audit involve?

A full inspection of your electrical installation against EPRA safety standards, with a written compliance report you can submit to regulators or insurers.

How often should a facility be audited?

Annual audits are standard for most commercial and industrial facilities; higher-risk sites may need more frequent inspection.

Can you audit a system another contractor installed?

Yes — audits are independent of who installed the system.
