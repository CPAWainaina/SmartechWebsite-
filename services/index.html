Services Overview

Solar, CCTV, electrical, automation, networking and security services

Smartech Technologies integrates power, security and connectivity so clients can procure, install and maintain critical infrastructure through one accountable technical team.

What does Smartech Technologies do?

Smartech designs, supplies, installs and supports solar power, battery backup, CCTV, access control, electric fencing, electrical distribution, industrial automation, control systems, structured cabling, networking and selected safety systems.

Where does Smartech operate?

Kenya is the primary local market, with a focus on Nairobi and wider project coverage. South Sudan work is handled on a project basis according to scope, logistics and site access.

Who is the service for?

Residential property owners, commercial buildings, farms, warehouses, offices, institutions, NGOs, project sites and facilities teams.

How does a project start?

Start with the property location, the problem to solve, existing equipment and your target outcome. Smartech can then conduct a site assessment and develop a specification and quotation.
